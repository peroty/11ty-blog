# About Me

## Hi, I'm Carl Holscher

You probably know me as **peroty**.

In a past life I've worked as a print designer, then I went to the web and designed there. I returned to my roots and worked at a print shop turning pixels into proofs. Then I fell into the world of tech support. From there I've worked in city, state and Federal governments supporting their staffs. I've worked for The Atlantic magazine, Honeywell and General Electric. Now, I work on remote collaboration technologies as a Rich Media Engineer.

I'm a writer and photographer who loves exploring technology, but I prefer simple solutions over complex ones. That's why I built this blog with [11ty](https://www.11ty.dev/) - a static site generator that gets out of the way and lets me focus on creating content.

## What I Write About

On this blog, you'll find:

- **Posts**: Long-form articles about technology, web development, and my creative process
- **Notes**: Quick thoughts and updates
- **Links**: Interesting things I find around the web with my commentary
- **Photos**: Visual stories and photography work

## Podcasts
